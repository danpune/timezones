Time Zones

Drag the hours or tap the time to type one. Tap ☆ to set the base city, hold and drag it to reorder.

    Why this exists

    I wanted a simple way to compare a handful of time zones at a glance, and to relate them back to whatever time it is where I am. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking.

    Conversions come from your own device's time zone database, so daylight saving is always current. Sunrise, sunset and the sky colours are computed from each city's latitude and longitude rather than fetched, and the page caches itself, so it keeps working with no connection. MIT licensed.

    How to use it

    • Reorder cities: press and hold a city's ☆, then drag it up or down. Or tap Reorder and use ▲ ▼.
    • Set your base city: tap its ☆. The clock and slider follow that city.
    • Pick a time: drag across the hours, or tap the time and type one, like 3pm or 15:30.
    • Rename a city: tap its name, say “Mom” or “HQ”.
    • Add or remove: start typing a city or country and pick from the list; tap × to remove one.
    • Share: tap Copy link to send this exact view, or Copy times for a text summary.
    • Keep it on your phone: add it to your home screen. It works offline.
